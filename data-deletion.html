Data deletion instructions

Last updated: 24 October 2026

You can ask us to delete your Hebelie account and personal data we hold. This page satisfies Meta/Facebook requirements for user data deletion when you use Facebook Login or other sign-in methods.

What we delete

When we process a valid request, we delete or anonymize, where applicable:

  • Your authentication account (Google, Facebook, or email sign-in) in our auth system.
  • Stored preview records linked to your account (treatment choices, preview URLs).
  • Consultation or lead form submissions tied to your account or email.

We may retain minimal records if required by law or for fraud prevention, and we may keep aggregated, non-identifying statistics.

How to request deletion

  1. Email privacy@hebelie.com with the subject line Hebelie data deletion request.
  2. Include the email address you used to sign in (or the email from your Facebook/Google account). If you used Facebook Login only, state that and include your Facebook account email if available.
  3. We will confirm receipt and complete deletion within 30 days, unless a longer period is required by law.

Facebook Login users

Deleting your data in Hebelie does not delete your Facebook account. To remove Hebelie’s access in Facebook, go to Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, select Hebelie, and remove the connection. You should still email us so we delete data on our side.

Without an account

If you never completed sign-in, portrait data you only kept in your browser is not stored on our servers. Clear your browser site data for hebelie.com to remove local copies.
