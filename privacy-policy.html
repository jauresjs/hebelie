Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026 · Applies to hebelie.com

Hebelie (“we”, “us”) provides an online simulation so you can preview aesthetic treatments on your own portrait before a consultation. This policy describes what we collect, why we use it, and your choices.

Who we are

Hebelie operates the website at hebelie.com. For privacy requests contact privacy@hebelie.com.

What we collect

  • Account (sign-in): If you sign in with Google, Facebook, or email magic link, our authentication provider (Supabase) stores your account identifier and, when available, your name and email from that provider.
  • Portrait: When you upload or capture a photo, it is resized in your browser. It is sent to our servers only when you generate a preview, solely to produce that simulation.
  • Preview & preferences: Treatments selected, preview strength, and the generated preview image URL may be stored with your account.
  • Lead requests: If you choose “Talk to a professional” or submit a consultation form, we store the contact details you provide.
  • Technical: Standard logs from our host and auth provider (e.g. IP address, browser type, timestamps) for security and reliability.

How we use data

  • To authenticate you and run AI preview generation you request.
  • To save previews and optional consultation requests linked to your account.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the service.
  • We do not use your portrait or previews to train third-party AI models.

Facebook Login

If you use Facebook Login, Meta may process data according to its own policies. We receive from Facebook (via Supabase) what is needed to create your Hebelie session—typically your Facebook user id and, if you grant permission, your name and email. We do not post to Facebook on your behalf.

Sharing

We use service providers that process data on our instructions, including Supabase (auth and database), our AI image provider (preview generation), and static hosting. We do not sell personal data.

Retention

Portraits are used for the generation flow and associated preview records; we aim to minimize retention of raw uploads. Account and preview metadata are kept while your account exists or as needed for legal and operational purposes. You may request deletion—see our Data deletion instructions.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict use of your personal data. Contact privacy@hebelie.com. EU/UK users may also lodge a complaint with their supervisory authority.

Children

Hebelie is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
