For owners & managers

The questions before you install.

We already have a contact form. Why this?

Forms only catch people ready to identify themselves—often 10–20% of visitors. The other 80–90% browse and leave. A treatment preview is something they’ll actually do. The consult request is the form, except you already know lips vs. jawline vs. toxin.

Do you steal our patients?

No marketplace. The embed lives on your domain experience, booking goes to your link, and leads sit in your dashboard. Hebelie is infrastructure, not a directory.

Is the picture a guaranteed result?

No. It’s a directional illustration so the consult starts further along. Healing, product, and your clinician’s technique decide the real outcome. Say that on your site; we say it in the product.
